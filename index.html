Our Team

A community of women who love to run

We're women ages 35+ who love to race and who are open to trying new things, with an emphasis on distances from the 400m up to the half marathon.

Each athlete has her own training, schedule, and goals, and we come together for key races throughout the year and for monthly team runs. We aim to keep it fun and support each other every step of the way.

Who We Are
  • Women in New England (generally the greater Boston area), ages 35+, who love to train and race.
  • Competitive in both the masters and open divisions, but there are no performance requirements for the team.
  • Driven by a love for running and racing, embracing new challenges while keeping things fun and grounded.
The Commitment

Key races and monthly runs

Key races

We come together for several races each year, which can change year to year. Examples:

  • Boston 10K For Women
  • Cambridge Half Marathon
  • New Balance Indoor Grand Prix Masters Mile
  • James Joyce Ramble 10K
  • Cambridge Spring Classic 5K

Track relays

We'll also try to field 4x400 and 4x800 relay teams at track meets such as Penn Relays, Millrose Games, and USATF Masters Nationals.

Monthly team runs

Rotating locations across the greater Boston and Providence area. We're there for each other during races, training, and everything in between.

Minimal dues

Just enough to cover the USATF club fee and the cost of a racing singlet.
